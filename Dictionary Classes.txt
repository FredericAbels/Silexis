	nP = set of words with n phonemes active.

	4 phonemes per digit

		|2P| = 4x4 
			= 16
		|3P| = (4 choose 2)*4 
			+ 4*(4 choose 2) 
			= 2*6*4 
			= 48
		|4P| = 2*(4 choose 3)*4 
			+ (4 choose 2)*(4 choose 2)
			= 2*4*4 + 6*6
			= 64

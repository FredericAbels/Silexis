	nP = set of words with N phonemes active.
	|2P| = 4x4 
		 = 16
	|3P| = (4 choose 2)*4 
		 + 4*(4 choose 2) 
		 = 2*6*4 
		 = 48
	|4P| = 2*(4 choose 3)*4 
		 + (4 choose 2)*(4 choose 2)
		 = 2*4*4 + 6*6
		 = 64
		
	sum = 128
	That makes 12.800 one-morpheme words.

	uneconomical since the smallest two morpheme words have 4 phonemes. I could use this space for niche philosophical words or something.
		|5P| = 2*1*4
			+ 2*(4 choose 3)*(4 choose 2)
			= 8 + 48
			= 56
		|6P| = (4 choose 2)*1*2
			+ (4 choose 3)*2
			= 12 + 8
			= 20
